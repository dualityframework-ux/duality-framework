How it works. Gross accuracy is 1 − (sum of absolute variance dollars ÷ book value); net accuracy lets overages and shortages offset. Each variance line is checked against common causes in order: mislocated stock (the same SKU over in one slot and short in another), unit-of-measure errors (exact pack multiples), transposed digits, look-alike swaps in the same aisle, timing (movement just before the count), empty and found locations, and counters with an unusually high variance rate.
