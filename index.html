Method

Result = process + variance.

Every tool here follows the same three steps, the same way I root-cause an inventory variance at work.

  1. Observation

    State what happened, plainly, with the number.

  2. Mechanism

    Find the driver underneath it, and separate it from noise.

  3. Implication

    Say what it means for the next decision.

Observation: Variance’s synthetic sample count is 99.01% accurate on net. Mechanism: overages and shortages cancel; gross accuracy is 94.32%, and 9 of 28 variance lines carry 80% of the gross dollars. Implication: research those 9 lines first, and confirm each suggested cause with a recount before adjusting. See the numbers
